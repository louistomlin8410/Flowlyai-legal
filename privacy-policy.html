Flowly AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09/11/2026

1. Information We Collect

Flowly AI ("Flowly AI", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how it is stored, and the rights available to you when you use the Flowly AI app.

We collect information that you provide when creating and using your Flowly AI account, information generated through your use of the app, and certain technical information collected automatically.

Account Information

Depending on how you sign in, we may collect the following information:

  • Name: Used to personalise the app, display your name, and identify your account.
  • Email address: Used to create and manage your account, verify your email address, send password-reset emails, and contact you when necessary.
  • Firebase UID: A unique identifier used to associate your account with your tasks, schedules, preferences, and other app data.
  • Sign-in provider: Information about whether you use email/password, Google, or Microsoft to sign in.
  • Account creation date: Used to manage account information and support certain app features and analytics.

When you use email/password authentication, your password is securely managed by Firebase Authentication. Flowly AI does not have access to or directly store your password.

If you use Google or Microsoft to sign in, we receive information required to authenticate your account, such as your name, email address, and unique account identifier, through the relevant authentication service.

Information You Provide in the App

Flowly AI may collect information that you enter into the app, including:

  • Short biography or bio
  • Interests
  • Occupation
  • Tasks and task information
  • Schedule information
  • Scheduling preferences
  • Goals
  • Wake-up and bedtime preferences
  • Weekly availability
  • Task categories
  • Task completion information
  • Productivity and completed-task history
  • Preferences and settings, including notification preferences
  • Personal goals set by the user (only the goal name is collected)
  • Reminders and reminder information
  • Thoughts and thought-related information
  • Personal schedule format templates

Task information is collected to create, organise, and manage your schedules, provide AI-powered scheduling, display tasks on the calendar, send relevant task notifications, and track task completion and productivity.

Each task may also have a randomly generated task ID. This allows Flowly AI to correctly create, update, delete, and synchronise tasks.

2. AI-Powered Scheduling

Flowly AI uses the OpenAI API and GPT-5 to provide AI-powered scheduling.

When you request an AI-generated schedule, information relevant to creating your schedule may be sent to OpenAI for processing. This may include:

  • Occupation
  • Interests
  • Bio
  • Schedule date
  • Current local time
  • Wake-up time
  • Bedtime
  • Goals
  • Available hours
  • Energy level
  • Break preferences
  • Fixed events
  • Priority levels
  • Additional notes
  • Existing tasks and task details
  • Available time windows
  • Existing task windows
  • Working hours
  • Earliest permitted start time
  • Available-time budget
  • Allowed categories
  • Thoughts within the thoughtbox
  • Goals made by the user (only the name of the goal)

Flowly AI may also use relevant planning history, such as the number of schedules applied or completed, completed and incomplete task counts, total focused minutes, and commonly used task categories, to help personalise scheduling.

Only information relevant to providing the AI scheduling functionality should be sent to the AI service.

OpenAI processes information sent through the API to provide the AI service. API inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI models by default unless the customer explicitly opts in. OpenAI may retain certain API-related data for abuse monitoring and security purposes according to its policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through Flowly AI to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate you and provide sign-in services.
  • Verify your email address.
  • Provide password-reset functionality.
  • Store and synchronise your information across supported devices.
  • Personalise your Flowly AI experience.
  • Generate AI-powered schedules and recommendations.
  • Organise and display your tasks and schedules.
  • Provide notifications and reminders.
  • Track task completion and productivity features.
  • Improve the app and its features.
  • Understand how users interact with Flowly AI.
  • Monitor and improve app performance and stability.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical problems.
  • Protect our services from abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Provide customer and technical support.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Display and measure advertisements, manage advertising frequency, prevent invalid activity, and comply with advertising and privacy requirements.
  • Apply and remember advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

Legal Bases for Processing

Where UK data protection law requires us to identify a lawful basis for processing personal information, the basis will depend on the purpose and the feature being used. The bases that may apply include:

  • Contract: Processing information that is necessary to create and manage your account and provide the app features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: Processing that is necessary for purposes such as protecting the app from abuse, maintaining security, diagnosing technical problems, and improving reliability, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • Consent: Processing where we ask for your permission, including where consent is required for particular analytics or advertising-related technologies or activities. You can withdraw consent where processing is based on it.
  • Legal obligation: Processing that is necessary for us to comply with applicable legal requirements.

Some third-party providers, including Google in connection with its advertising services, may determine their own purposes and lawful bases for processing information. Their privacy information explains their processing in more detail.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Certain information may be collected automatically when you use Flowly AI through services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase App Check, and Google AdMob.

This may include:

  • App interaction and usage events
  • Device and system information
  • Engagement information
  • Crash and error information
  • Crash stack traces
  • Relevant application-state information
  • Technical identifiers
  • Advertising-related information, which may include an advertising identifier, ad interactions, and information used to deliver or measure ads
  • App and device verification information
  • Consent and privacy-choice information relating to advertising, where applicable

Flowly AI uses basic Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app and improve features, performance, and user experience. Flowly AI does not use Firebase Analytics to create personalised advertising profiles.

5. Third-Party Services

Flowly AI uses third-party services to provide and maintain parts of the app. These services may process information as described below.

Firebase Firestore

Firebase Firestore is used to store account, profile, task, schedule, and other app data.

Information stored may include your name, email address, bio, tasks, interests, occupation, account creation information, Firebase UID, and other information required for Flowly AI to function.

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, verify email addresses, provide password resets, and manage sign-in providers.

It may process your email address, authentication provider, Firebase UID, and other authentication-related information.

Passwords are securely managed by Firebase Authentication and are not accessible to Flowly AI.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how users interact with Flowly AI and helps us improve features, performance, and the overall user experience.

It may process app usage information, interaction events, device and system information, and engagement information.

Flowly AI uses basic analytics and does not use this information to create personalised advertising profiles.

Google AdMob and Advertising

Flowly AI uses Google AdMob, a Google advertising service, to display advertisements in the app. Depending on your location, device settings, applicable law, and the privacy choices you make, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

To provide, limit, measure, and protect the delivery of advertisements, Google AdMob and its related services may process information such as:

  • Device information, including device model, operating system, and language;
  • IP address and information that may be used to estimate a general location;
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers, where available;
  • Information about the app and how advertisements are displayed or interacted with;
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and other advertising measurement information;
  • Technical information used for ad delivery, frequency capping, security, and fraud prevention;
  • Consent signals and advertising privacy choices, where applicable.

The information processed can vary depending on your device, your settings, the advertising features in use, and the choices you make. Flowly AI does not use Firebase Analytics to build personalised advertising profiles. Advertising-related processing by Google AdMob is separate from that statement and is governed by Google's services and the choices available to you.

Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Flowly AI uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required. The app requests updated consent information and displays a consent message when Google determines that one is required for your location or circumstances.

The consent message may explain the relevant advertising and data-processing choices available to you. The options shown can vary by location and applicable requirements. You can make a choice using the message presented in the app. Flowly AI uses those choices when determining whether it may request advertisements, subject to the applicable technical and legal requirements.

If Google requires the app to provide a privacy-options entry point, you can use the app's Ad privacy choices setting to revisit the available choices. This option may only be displayed when it is required or made available by Google's consent tools.

Choosing not to allow personalised advertising does not necessarily mean that no advertisements will be shown. Where permitted, non-personalised advertisements may still be displayed. Some information may still be processed for purposes such as ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, as permitted by applicable law and your choices.

Google processes information under its own privacy terms and policies. For more information about how Google uses information in advertising, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy .

Firebase App Check

Flowly AI uses Firebase App Check to help protect its backend services against abuse by verifying that requests are coming from an authorised version of the app or an eligible device environment. This may involve processing app, device, and verification information, including attestation or integrity signals. This information is used for security and abuse prevention and is not intended to identify you by your name.

Google Sign-In

Google services allow users to sign in using their Google account.

Google may provide Flowly AI with information required for authentication, such as your name, email address, and unique account identifier.

Microsoft Sign-In

Microsoft services allow users to sign in using their Microsoft account.

Microsoft may provide Flowly AI with information required for authentication, such as your name, email address, and unique account identifier.

OpenAI

Flowly AI uses the OpenAI API and GPT-5 to provide AI-powered scheduling.

Information relevant to generating a schedule may be sent to OpenAI when you use AI scheduling features, as described in the AI-Powered Scheduling section of this Privacy Policy.

OpenAI processes this information to provide the requested AI service.

Firebase Crashlytics

Flowly AI uses Firebase Crashlytics to monitor, diagnose, and fix app crashes and Android application-not-responding (ANR) events.

Crashlytics automatically processes technical information relevant to diagnosing these problems, which may include crash stack traces, relevant application-state information, device hardware specifications, and Crashlytics installation identifiers.

This information helps us identify and prioritise technical problems and improve the stability and performance of Flowly AI.

6. Data Storage and Security

User account and app data that Flowly AI stores is stored using Firebase services.

Firebase Authentication manages authentication information, while Firebase Firestore stores tasks, profile information, interests, occupation, schedules, and other app data.

Storing this information allows users to access their Flowly AI account and associated information across supported devices.

We use reasonable security measures designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections between the app and our services, user authentication, and restrictions on access to user data by authorised services required for Flowly AI to function.

Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and are not stored or accessible by Flowly AI.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the service, maintaining account records, protecting the app, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. The period depends on the type of information and why it is being processed.

If you delete your account, we will initiate deletion of the account and user-specific information described in the Account Deletion section. Some limited information may remain for a period in backups, security records, or technical logs before it is overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course of those systems, or where retention is required or permitted by law. Third-party providers may retain information in accordance with their own retention policies.

We do not state a fixed deletion period for backups or third-party records because those periods can depend on the systems and providers involved.

International Data Transfers

Some of the service providers used by Flowly AI may process information in countries outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, the relevant provider's infrastructure, contractual terms, and transfer arrangements may apply. Where required by UK data protection law, appropriate safeguards will be used for restricted transfers, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

For further information about a provider's processing locations and transfer safeguards, you can consult that provider's privacy and data protection documentation or contact us using the details below.

8. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Flowly AI account from within the app.

To delete your account, go to:

Profile → Settings → Account → Profile → Delete Account

You will be asked to confirm before your account is deleted.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, Flowly AI will delete your Firebase Authentication account and remove the user-specific information stored by Flowly AI, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Tasks
  • Bio
  • Interests
  • Occupation
  • Account information
  • Firebase UID
  • Other user-specific data associated with your account

If you cannot access your account or cannot delete it through the app, you can contact us at support.flowlyai@gmail.com for assistance.

Age-related protections and advertising settings may also depend on the app's declared audience, the user's location, and applicable platform and legal requirements. We will configure these settings to reflect the audience Flowly AI is made available to.

A parent or guardian who believes that a child under 13 has provided personal information to Flowly AI can contact us at support.flowlyai@gmail.com and request its deletion.
