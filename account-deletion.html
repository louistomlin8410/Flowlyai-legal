Flowly AI Account Deletion

Last updated: october 22, 2026

Flowly AI allows users to permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Flowly AI account directly from within the app.

To delete your account:

  1. Open Flowly AI.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open the Privacy section.
  4. Select Account Deletion.
  5. Follow the instructions to permanently delete your account.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, Flowly AI will delete your Firebase Authentication account and remove the personal data associated with your account that is stored by Flowly AI.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Tasks
  • Schedules
  • Bio
  • Interests
  • Occupation
  • Account information
  • Firebase UID
  • Other user-specific information associated with your account

Unable to Access Your Account?

If you cannot access your Flowly AI account and therefore cannot delete it through the app, you can contact us and request account deletion.

Account deletion requests:

support.flowlyai@gmail.com

We may need to verify that you are the account owner before processing a deletion request.

Questions About Account Deletion
